# Research Plan - PodEdit

## Research Questions

**RQ1:** How accurately can cross-correlation sync multi-camera podcast footage compared to manual syncing?

**RQ2:** What is the precision and recall of amplitude-based silence detection across different audio qualities?

**RQ3:** How much time does automated editing save compared to manual editing?

**RQ4:** Does our open-source approach achieve comparable quality to commercial tools (Descript, TimeBolt)?

**RQ5 (if adding ML):** How does speaker diarization improve camera switching accuracy?
